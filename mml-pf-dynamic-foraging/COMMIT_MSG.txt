Two-pass subject-level volatility, resampling fixes, repo hygiene

Scoring
-------
block_cv.score_session gains `sigmas=` (use a given volatility instead of
fitting one) and `fit_only=` (return the fitted volatility and skip the GLM
and the scored pass). hpc_block_cv.run_task uses both to score in two passes:
pass 1 fits a volatility per (session, placement), pass 2 takes the median
across the subject's placements and re-scores everything at that shared value.

The motivation is measured, not assumed. On sessions simulated with a TRUE
volatility of exactly zero, the per-session estimate ranged over [0.001, 0.400]
-- essentially the whole search interval -- with a median of 0.13. The
likelihood surface in sigma is nearly flat and what the optimiser returns is
dominated by session-level noise. Taking the median across a subject's sessions
cuts the across-subject spread by roughly a factor of three (sd 0.019 vs 0.073
within-subject for sigma_alpha).

Pooling via a joint likelihood over sessions (block_cv.fit_pooled, included and
documented) was also tried and is WORSE at matched compute: across-subject sd
0.035 vs 0.019 for sigma_alpha, 0.049 vs 0.010 for sigma_beta. The median of
per-session fits wins because it averages; a single differential-evolution run
on a flat multimodal surface does not. fit_pooled is kept but is not the
default.

Results now record both volatilities: sigma_{alpha,beta,phi} is the shared
value used for scoring, sigma_*_session is that placement's own fit, and
n_sigma_fits is how many went into the median. The task stage prints the
across-placement range, which is the quantity that should be reported -- a
sigma whose range spans the search interval is unidentified, not estimated.

Resampling
----------
mmlpf_masked gains `resample=` ("systematic" or "multinomial") and
`resample_threshold=` (resample only when the effective sample size falls
below threshold*N). Fitting and scoring now default to systematic with
threshold 0.5; mmlpf_masked itself still defaults to multinomial-every-trial,
so any direct caller reproduces exactly. Verified byte-identical at the old
defaults: NLL agreeing to ten decimals and every collected array, including
the particle histograms, unchanged.

Systematic resampling halves the bias in the volatility estimate at every
particle count tested (median sigma_alpha 0.150/0.130/0.130 -> 0.060/0.060/
0.065 at N = 100/400/1600, against a truth of zero).

Two earlier hypotheses were tested and REJECTED, and the code reflects that:
sigma does not fall with particle count (flat from N=100 to N=1600 at six
seeds), and selecting sigma by held-out rather than training likelihood picks
the same value, so cross-validating the hyperparameter would change nothing.
The practical cost of a mis-chosen volatility is small -- the held-out
likelihood is nearly flat in sigma -- so this is an interpretability fix, not
a predictive one.

Resume semantics (BREAKING for existing per-subject CSVs)
--------------------------------------------------------
The resume unit is now the SUBJECT, not the placement. A complete CSV is
skipped; a partial one is moved aside as *_partial_<timestamp>.csv and the
subject is refit whole. Resuming placement-by-placement would leave some rows
scored under one shared volatility and the rest under another -- two models
silently mixed in one file, the same failure mode as the earlier schema drift.
The four new columns also trip the existing schema guard, so pre-existing
per-subject CSVs are renamed rather than appended to.

Terminology
-----------
Renamed all 13 occurrences of "M-step" across seven files to "volatility fit".
There is no E-step and no expected complete-data likelihood: this is a direct
maximum marginal likelihood fit of the volatilities, with differential
evolution outside and the particle filter supplying the likelihood estimate.
The EM wording would not survive a methods section.

Repo hygiene
------------
Added .gitignore (bytecode, caches, resume backups, scratch figures at the
repo root -- data/ figures stay tracked), requirements.txt, and README.md.

requirements.txt records a non-obvious constraint for the cluster container:
compute nodes need psytrack and matplotlib even though no compute job calls
them, because `import hpc_block_cv` reaches `mmlpf_vs_psytrack_cv`, which
imports both at module level. Omitting either kills every array task at
import.

Removed (to Trash): __pycache__/ including stale .pyc files for modules that
no longer exist, .DS_Store, and two regenerable test caches, cache_demo/
(synthetic) and cache_lap/ (12 subjects). cache50/ is kept.

Pruned 12 superseded scripts: check_progress, construct_validity,
drift_dynamic_recovery, local_cohort_comparable_validation, matched_validation,
merge_and_plot_cohort, pf_plots, plot_cross_validation_nll,
plot_single_trajectory, session_model_comparison, solution_comparison,
two_subject_comparison.

matched_validation was load-bearing: block_cv, latent_comparison,
engagement_comparison, bias_model_comparison and bias_showcase all imported it
at module level, so deleting it outright would have broken every array task at
import. Each of them used exactly one function and nothing else, so
build_glm_regressors (25 lines) moved into block_cv.py beside the GLM it feeds,
and those four callers now import it from there. No circular import is created
-- all four already imported block_cv. Verified: all eight pipeline modules
import with matched_validation absent, and build_glm_regressors returns the
same shapes with the session-boundary zeroing intact.

empirical_drifting_agent_mml_pf.py is deliberately NOT removed. Nothing
imports it any more, but block_cv's particle dynamics, parameterisations and
clip bounds were copied from it verbatim; it is the provenance for the whole
comparison.

Results consolidated into data/
-------------------------------
Every CSV at the repo root moved into data/ -- these are fitting results:
small, slow to regenerate, and in two cases unreproducible.
local_cohort_comparable_validation.csv in particular can no longer be
regenerated at all, since its generating script is gone.

Four figures kept and moved: showcase_{707254,707535,708299}_real.png and
showcase_708299_v2_real.png. 707535 is the only real-data win, 707254 and
708299 are the two documented losses that make it credible, and the
708299 v1/v2 pair is the before/after evidence for the parameterisation fix --
the v1 half cannot be regenerated now that block_cv is PARAM_VERSION 2.

Ten figures removed: bias_comparison.png and bias_parameterization.png
(regenerable with --summarize from the CSVs now in data/), the four
bias_showcase_* figures (superseded by the named showcase_* runs), the three
showcase_synth_* figures (synthetic, i.e. simulated from the model under test),
and latent_comparison_648586_s2.png (regenerable with latent_comparison.py).

Not comparable to earlier results
---------------------------------
Everything in data/ predates PARAM_VERSION 2 and the resampling change. Do not
pool old and new cohort numbers.
